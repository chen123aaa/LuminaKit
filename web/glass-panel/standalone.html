LuminaKit

Frosted glass, in plain CSS

A frosted panel in the spirit of Apple's materials: heavy backdrop blur with a saturation boost, a refracted band along the rim, a crisp specular hairline, fine grain so large areas do not band, and layered elevation. One class, no dependencies — the script is only for the pointer sheen.

Usage

Just a div

Paste the two component blocks from this file, then use <div class="lg-panel">. Add --thin / --thick for other weights, --light on light surfaces, and --sheen for the pointer highlight.
